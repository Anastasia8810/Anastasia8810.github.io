Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Write to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). We usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the problem.

## Frequently asked questions

### The spotter doesn’t react to my voice

1. Check that **Microphone** and **Speech Recognition** are allowed: **Settings → Baller**.
2. Speak clearly and use a command: “start workout”, “next exercise”, “pause”, “resume”, “repeat”, “finish”.
3. The spotter understands English and Russian. In a noisy gym, keep your iPhone closer or use headphones with a microphone.

### How do I add a player or a coach to my team?

Open the **Team** tab and show your QR code, or share an invite link. Your teammate scans the code with the in-app scanner or opens the link, then taps **Join**.

### I don’t receive team calls

Check that the app is allowed to use the microphone and that your phone isn’t in Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode that silences calls. Open the app at least once with an internet connection so your phone can receive calls.

### Where are my workout recordings?

Recordings are stored on your iPhone inside the app, in the recordings list. To keep a copy elsewhere, save the recording to Photos from the app. Deleting the app deletes recordings that weren’t saved to Photos.

### Does the app analyze my shot automatically?

Not yet. You can already record and review your shooting form. Automatic shot analysis is in development.

### How do I delete my data?

Data on your iPhone is removed when you delete the app. To delete data kept on our server (name, team roster, avatar, undelivered messages), email us from the address above. Details are in the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

### Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later.
